Popular Places

Liberia has many tourist attractions including beaches, waterfalls, national parks, and historical sites. Robertsport is famous for surfing and beautiful coastal scenery.

Sapo National Park is home to many species of wildlife and provides opportunities for eco-tourism and nature exploration.

Visitors can also explore Providence Island, an important historical site connected to Liberia's founding.
